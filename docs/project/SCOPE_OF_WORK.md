## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Purpose
The REEMA Multi-Agent Documentation System is an advanced AI-powered platform designed to automate the creation of comprehensive IT process documentation. The system employs 9 specialized Claude AI agents working in a hierarchical architecture to generate professional Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that comply with ITIL, ITOM, ITAM, and other IT industry standards.

### 1.2 Scope of Services
The system provides end-to-end automated documentation services for:
- **ITIL Core Processes:** Incident Management, Change Management, Problem Management, Service Request Management
- **IT Operations Management (ITOM):** Monitoring, Event Management, Infrastructure Operations
- **IT Asset Management (ITAM):** Asset Lifecycle, Configuration Management, License Management
- **Security & Compliance:** Security Operations, Audit Management, Risk Management
- **Custom IT Processes:** Organization-specific workflows and procedures

### 1.3 Deliverables
Each documentation project produces:
- Comprehensive 15-30 page SOP documents
- Multi-format outputs: PDF, Microsoft Word (DOCX), Markdown, HTML
- Power BI dashboard design specifications
- Analytics frameworks and KPI visualization strategies
- RACI matrices and role definitions
- Process flow diagrams and workflows
- Compliance checklists and audit requirements

## 4. WORKFLOW & PROCESS

### 4.1 Documentation Generation Workflow

**Phase 1: Request & Planning**
1. Khalid (Owner) initiates documentation request
2. Norah (Secretary) receives and coordinates the request
3. Norah relays requirements to Abdullah (Leader)
4. Abdullah plans workflow and assigns tasks

**Phase 2: Research & Analysis** (Parallel Execution)
5. Taif conducts ITIL/ITOM research
6. Mohammad performs standards and compliance analysis
7. Both provide findings to Abdullah

**Phase 3: Content Generation**
8. Saad generates comprehensive 15-30 page SOP content
9. Layla creates analytics framework and Power BI dashboard designs
10. Outputs integrated by Abdullah

**Phase 4: Quality Review**
11. Faisal reviews content for quality (85% threshold)
12. If quality < 85%, feedback sent to Saad for refinement
13. Iterative refinement until quality standards met

**Phase 5: Formatting & Export**
14. Bee converts approved content to multiple formats (PDF, DOCX, MD, HTML)
15. Abdullah assembles final deliverables

**Phase 6: Approval & Delivery**
16. Abdullah submits to Norah for executive review
17. Norah coordinates Owner (Khalid) approval
18. Final documentation released to stakeholders

### 4.2 Quality Gates
- **Research Gate:** Comprehensive ITIL/standards research completed
- **Content Gate:** 15-30 page SOP with all 11 sections complete
- **Quality Gate:** 85% minimum quality score from Faisal
- **Format Gate:** All requested formats successfully generated
- **Executive Gate:** Owner (Khalid) final approval
